# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Everyone who works in the **sunshine-realty-brokerage-inc** organization (employees,
contractors, and partners) is expected to keep the workplace professional, respectful,
and free from harassment, whatever a person's age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

This Code of Conduct supplements, and does not replace, Sunshine Realty Brokerage Inc.'s
workplace policies and applicable law, including the *Ontario Human Rights Code* and the
*Occupational Health and Safety Act*.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Protect confidential company and client information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission
- Sharing confidential company, client, or listing information outside authorized channels
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all organization spaces: repositories, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any communication related to the organization's work.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to an organization owner, to your manager, or to Sunshine
Realty Brokerage Inc. through [sunshinerealtybrokerage.ca](https://sunshinerealtybrokerage.ca).
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept as confidential as possible. Retaliation against
anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity, violations may result in a warning, removal of content, loss
of repository or organization access, and disciplinary action under company policy, up to
and including termination of employment or contract.
