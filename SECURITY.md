# Security Policy

Sunshine Realty Brokerage Inc. takes the security of our systems, our clients' data, and
our listing information seriously. This policy applies to every repository in the
**sunshine-realty-brokerage-inc** organization.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Do not report security issues through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.**

Report them privately using one of these channels:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting.** Where it is enabled, open the repository's
   **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
2. **Organization owners.** Members can contact an organization owner directly.
3. **Company contact.** Everyone else can reach us through
   [sunshinerealtybrokerage.ca](https://sunshinerealtybrokerage.ca).

Please include:

- The affected repository, service, or URL
- A description of the issue and its possible impact
- Steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
- Any logs, screenshots, or other supporting detail

## What to Expect

| Stage                        | Target time                    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Acknowledgement of report    | Within 2 business days         |
| Initial assessment           | Within 5 business days         |
| Status updates               | At least weekly until resolved |
| Fix for critical issues      | As soon as possible            |

## Scope

In scope: all code, infrastructure, and applications owned by Sunshine Realty Brokerage Inc.

Out of scope: third-party services we use (report those to the vendor), social engineering
of staff, physical attacks, and denial-of-service testing.

## Rules for Testing

- Only test systems you are authorized to test.
- Do not access, change, or delete data that does not belong to you.
- Stop and report right away if you come across personal or client information.
- Keep the issue confidential until we confirm it is fixed.

## Leaked Secrets or Data

If you find credentials, API keys, client records, or personal information committed to
any repository, **do not copy or use them**. Notify an organization owner immediately so
the secrets can be revoked and the history cleaned.

## Handling Personal Information

Our systems process personal information covered by Canada's *Personal Information
Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial legislation.
Never commit real client, buyer, seller, or tenant data to a repository. Use anonymized or
synthetic data for development and testing.
